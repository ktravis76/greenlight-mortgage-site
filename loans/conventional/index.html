The honest part

What's actually stopping you?

01

“I need 20% down, right?”

No. That number is where private mortgage insurance falls away on a conventional loan — not the cost of entry. Qualifying first-time buyers can see programs starting at 3%. Waiting years to hit 20% has its own price tag: rent.

02

“I don't want to pay PMI forever.”

On conventional loans you don't. PMI comes off once you've built enough equity — unlike FHA, where it usually rides for the life of the loan. That difference is exactly why this program exists.

03

“My income is complicated.”

Self-employed, commission, two jobs — complicated isn't a no, it's a documentation question. And when one lender's box doesn't fit, we have a network of others. Options win.

Questions

Straight answers

How much do I actually need to put down?

Less than most people assume. Qualifying first-time buyers can see programs as low as 3%. What you should put down is a different question from what you must.

When does PMI go away?

Once you've built sufficient equity. Timing depends on your loan and how the value moves.

Is conventional always better than FHA?

No. For lower credit or a thinner down payment, FHA is frequently the better deal. It depends entirely on your file.
