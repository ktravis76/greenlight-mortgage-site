Why a broker

One bank has one menu. We have a network.

A loan officer at a bank can only offer you that bank's programs at that bank's pricing. If your file does not fit, the answer is no, and that is the end of the conversation.

We are a brokerage. We take the same file to a network of lenders and come back with what is actually available. That matters most on exactly the files banks find awkward — self-employed income, credit that is recovering, a rural address, a jumbo amount, a VA benefit nobody explained properly.
