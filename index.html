How it works

Loan options

Whatever the situation actually is.

First house, fifth house, self-employed, rebuilding credit, or just tired of the payment you have. There is usually a path.

Conventional

The standard route for buyers with steady income and reasonable credit. Often the lowest total cost when you qualify.

FHA

Lower down payment and more flexible credit requirements. A common first step for first-time buyers.

VA

For eligible veterans and service members. No down payment required in many cases and no monthly mortgage insurance.

USDA

Rural and small-town properties across East Texas. More of Gregg and Harrison County qualifies than people expect.

Refinance

Lower the payment, shorten the term, or pull cash out of equity. Worth a look any time the market moves.

VA IRRRL

The streamline refinance for veterans already in a VA loan. Less paperwork than a full refinance.

A note on numbers: Program availability and terms depend on your full application. Nothing on this page is a rate quote or an approval. Your licensed loan officer will walk you through what you actually qualify for.
Why a broker

The difference is who we work for.

A loan officer at a bank has one menu — the bank's. If your file does not fit that menu, you hear no. We are not stuck with one menu, so a no from one lender is just the start of the conversation.

That matters most for the people banks tend to turn away first: self-employed borrowers, folks rebuilding credit, anyone whose income does not fit neatly on a W-2.
