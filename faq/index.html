Buying your first home

How much do I need for a down payment?

Less than most people think. VA and USDA loans can be zero down. FHA starts at 3.5% with a 580 credit score, and some conventional loans start at 3%. The 20% you keep hearing about is a myth for most buyers. Which one fits depends on your credit, income and the home.

What credit score do I need to buy a house?

There is no single magic number. FHA generally works with scores of 580 and up, and sometimes lower with more down. Conventional and VA requirements depend on the loan and the lender. Because we are a broker, we can shop lenders whose guidelines fit your score instead of forcing you into one bank's rules.

How much house can I afford?

Lenders compare your monthly debts to your gross income, including the full house payment: principal, interest, taxes, insurance and any HOA. The amount you are approved for and the amount you are comfortable paying are not always the same, and we will show you both. Start with our affordability tool, then let's talk it through.

What is the difference between pre-qualified and pre-approved?

A pre-qualification is a quick estimate. A pre-approval means a lender has actually reviewed your credit and finances, and sellers and agents take it much more seriously. Neither one is a final commitment to lend. That comes after underwriting and an appraisal.

Will getting pre-approved hurt my credit?

Barely, and shopping around will not make it worse. Mortgage credit checks made within a short window, 14 to 45 days depending on the scoring model, count as a single inquiry. Compare lenders without worrying.

How much are closing costs?

Commonly about 2% to 5% of the purchase price, covering lender fees, title, appraisal and prepaid items like taxes and insurance. You get an itemized Loan Estimate within three business days of applying, and your final Closing Disclosure at least three business days before closing. Seller credits and assistance programs can cover some or all of it.

Is there down payment help in Texas?

Yes. The Texas State Affordable Housing Corporation (TSAHC) and the Texas Department of Housing and Community Affairs (TDHCA) both run programs, and the City of Longview has assistance for qualifying buyers. Income limits and rules apply and change, so ask us which ones you may qualify for.

Do I count as a first-time buyer if I owned a home before?

Often, yes. Most programs count you as first-time if you have not owned your primary home in the last three years. Some Texas programs waive that test for veterans.

How long does it take to buy a house?

A typical financed purchase takes about 30 to 45 days from signed contract to closing. Getting pre-approved before you shop is the easiest way to keep it on the short end.

VA loans

Do VA loans really require no down payment?

Yes. Eligible veterans with full entitlement can buy with zero down, and there is no VA loan limit for them. VA loans also have no monthly mortgage insurance, which quietly saves a lot over the life of the loan.

Am I eligible for a VA loan?

Generally, if you served 90 continuous days of active duty in wartime, 181 days in peacetime, or six years in the Guard or Reserve. Some surviving spouses qualify too. Your Certificate of Eligibility (COE) confirms it, and we can usually pull it for you in minutes.

What is the VA funding fee, and who does not pay it?

It is a one-time fee based on the loan amount and your down payment. Veterans who receive VA disability compensation do not pay it, and neither do some surviving spouses and active-duty Purple Heart recipients. If you are rated and nobody has asked you about it, ask us. The current fee table is on va.gov.

Can I use my VA loan more than once?

Yes. The benefit is reusable. Your entitlement can be restored when the loan is paid off, and in some cases you can have a second VA loan while keeping the first home.

Is a VA appraisal harder to pass?

The VA appraisal checks value and that the home is safe, sound and sanitary. It is not the deal-killer some sellers think it is, and a good lender and agent handle that conversation for you.

Can I get a VA loan with a lower credit score?

VA itself does not set a minimum score. Lenders do, and they differ. That is exactly where a broker helps.

Self-employed buyers

Can I get a mortgage if I am self-employed?

Yes. Traditional loans usually use two years of tax returns, which can be a problem if you write off a lot. Bank statement loans qualify you on your actual deposits instead.

How does a bank statement loan work?

The lender reviews 12 or 24 months of personal or business bank statements, adds up qualifying deposits, applies an expense factor for business accounts, and uses the result as your income. No tax returns are needed to qualify.

How much do I need down for a bank statement loan?

Commonly 10% to 20% on a primary home, and more for an investment property or a lower score. It depends on the program.

Why do bank statement loans cost more than regular loans?

They fall outside Fannie Mae and Freddie Mac rules, so investors price in the extra risk. For many self-employed buyers it is still the best way to buy now instead of waiting two more tax years.

Investment property and DSCR loans

What is a DSCR loan?

A loan for rental property that qualifies on the property's rent instead of your personal income. DSCR stands for debt service coverage ratio: the monthly rent divided by the monthly payment, including principal, interest, taxes, insurance and HOA. No tax returns or pay stubs needed.

What DSCR ratio do I need?

Many programs want 1.0 or higher, meaning the rent covers the payment. Higher ratios usually get better terms, and some programs go below 1.0 with more down.

How much down for an investment property loan?

Commonly 20% to 25% for a DSCR loan. It can be more depending on the ratio and your credit.

Can I close in my LLC?

Many DSCR programs allow it, usually with a personal guarantee.

Can I use Airbnb or short-term rental income?

Often, yes, with stricter rules: more down, a higher ratio, or rental history or market data to back it up. Check local and HOA rules first.

Working with a broker

What is the difference between a mortgage broker and a bank?

A bank lends its own money and offers its own products. A broker does not lend money. We shop your loan across a network of wholesale lenders to find the fit for your situation. More options, one point of contact.

How do mortgage brokers get paid?

A broker is paid either by the borrower or by the lender on a given loan, never both, and federal rules prohibit pay based on the loan's interest rate or terms. Every fee shows up on your Loan Estimate.

Is a broker cheaper than a bank?

Not automatically. Our advantage is access: many lenders, including programs most banks do not offer, like bank statement and DSCR loans. Whoever you use, compare Loan Estimates side by side.

Texas and East Texas

How much is the Texas homestead exemption?

The school-district homestead exemption is $140,000 off your home's taxable value, plus another $60,000 if you are 65 or older or disabled. Texas also caps how much a homestead's taxable value can rise each year at 10%.

When do I file for my homestead exemption?

After you own and live in the home. If you lived there on January 1, the deadline is April 30, and late filing is generally allowed for up to two years. In Gregg County, file with the Gregg Appraisal District at gcad.org.

How much cash can I take out of my home in Texas?

Texas limits all debt on your homestead to 80% of its value when you take cash out. That applies to cash-out refinances, home equity loans and HELOCs, including VA cash-out.

What is the 12-day rule on a Texas cash-out loan?

Texas makes you wait at least 12 days after applying, or after receiving the required Texas home equity notice if that is later, before you can close a cash-out loan on your homestead. Most fees are capped at 2% of the loan, and you can only do one every 12 months.

Is Longview eligible for USDA loans?

Most of the city of Longview is not, but much of the surrounding area is, including many rural parts of Gregg, Harrison, Upshur, Rusk and Smith counties. Eligibility goes by exact address and household income. Send us the address and we will check.

Can I finance land, acreage or a manufactured home?

Often, yes. Acreage depends on the appraisal and land-value limits. Manufactured homes usually need to be permanently attached to land you own and recorded as real property. Ask before you make an offer.

Why did my mortgage payment go up?

Usually escrow. Your lender reviews your escrow account every year, and when property taxes or insurance go up, so does the payment. Filing your homestead exemption is the easiest way to keep taxes down.

Refinancing

When does it make sense to refinance?

When the savings pay back the closing costs within the time you plan to stay. We will run your break-even before you spend a dime.

What is a VA IRRRL (VA streamline)?

A VA-to-VA refinance that lowers your rate with less paperwork, often no appraisal, and a 0.5% funding fee, waived if you receive VA disability compensation. It has to give you a real benefit, and you need at least six payments and 210 days on the current loan.

Can I get rid of FHA mortgage insurance by refinancing?

Often, yes. Once you have about 20% equity, refinancing from FHA into a conventional loan can remove mortgage insurance.
