The company

A brokerage, on purpose.

Greenlight is a mortgage brokerage, powered by Co/LAB Lending. That is a deliberate structure, not a technicality. A bank employs loan officers to sell that bank's products. A brokerage takes your file to a network of lenders and is free to recommend whichever one serves you best.

It also means we can say no to a loan. If refinancing will not pay for itself before you move, or if the program you asked about costs more than one you have not heard of, you will hear that from us.
